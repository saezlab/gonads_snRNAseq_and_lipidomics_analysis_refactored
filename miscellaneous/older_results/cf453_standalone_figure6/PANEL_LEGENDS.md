# CF453 Figure 6 panel legends

Panels are supplied separately for manual assembly. SD, standard diet; WD, Western diet; PP, periportal; PC, pericentral. RNA effects are sex associations, and lipids are measured in whole liver. **No individual primary gene–lipid correlation passes BH q≤0.05; all association panels are exploratory.**

Representative selection: St3gal6 (female PC); Auts2 (female PP); Unc5c (male PC); Hspa12a (male PP).

RNA primary q pools eligible genes × three interactions; within-sex zonation uses a distinct genes × four-contrast family. Lipid families and the single correlation family are defined in METHODS.md. Bootstrap summaries use fixed RNA/partner selections and are descriptive.

## Fig6A_SD

Female versus male moderated PP−PC t-statistics in SD; 18,486 genes, seven mice per sex. Positive is periportal and negative pericentral. Color requires primary sex-by-zone q≤0.05 and matching within-sex zonation q≤0.05; shared memberships are retained. Labels include representatives and up to eight genes ranked by interaction q. Axes show t-statistics, not fold changes.

Source data: [CSV](source_data/Fig6A_SD.csv).

## Fig6C_SD

Within-class lipid membership in SD. Class labels state all eligible measured lipid denominators. A context requires at least one diet-active RNA-candidate association with observed |r|≥0.65; multiple contexts denote shared membership and zero contexts denotes unassigned. The original C any-edge rule assigns 100% of eligible lipids in every class and both diets to multiple contexts (shared). This saturated summary provides no class-specific spatial discrimination. It is retained transparently; use the complementary pair-density panels to inspect continuous context differences. This any-edge summary is exploratory and does not localize lipids spatially.

Source data: [CSV](source_data/Fig6C_SD.csv).

## Fig6C_pair_density_SD

Exploratory pair density in SD: fraction of eligible union-candidate-gene × primary-lipid pairs with |r|≥0.65 within each lipid class and sex/zone. The candidate union and pair denominator are fixed across diets within each context; n=7 mice per estimate. All primary correlation q-values exceed 0.05. Density differences do not establish rewiring. Complete denominators and alternative summaries accompany the source table.

Source data: [CSV](source_data/Fig6C_pair_density_SD.csv).
