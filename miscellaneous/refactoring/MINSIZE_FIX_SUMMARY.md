# Pathway Enrichment minsize Parameter Fix

## Problem

Pathways present in the original analysis were missing from workflow 02 results. Specifically, `GOMF_ESTROGEN_2_HYDROXYLASE_ACTIVITY` was present when running the original `pseudobulk_pathways.R` but absent from `results/pseudobulk/pathways/pathway_scores_df_all.RData` generated by workflow 02.

## Root Cause

**Incorrect `minsize` parameters in enrichment analyses**

The `minsize` parameter in `decoupleR::run_ulm()` filters out gene sets/regulons with fewer than N genes. Using too large a minsize excludes small but biologically important pathways.

### Original vs. Workflow Parameters

| Analysis | Original Script | Workflow 02 (WRONG) | Workflow 02 (FIXED) |
|----------|----------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| **Pathway Enrichment** | No minsize specified<br>(default = 5) | `minsize = 15`<br>❌ Too restrictive | `minsize = 5`<br>✓ Matches original |
| **TF Activity** | `minsize = 20`<br>(explicitly set) | `minsize = 15`<br>❌ Too permissive | `minsize = 20`<br>✓ Matches original |

## Why This Matters

### Small but Important Gene Sets

Many hormone-related pathways have small gene sets:
- **GOMF_ESTROGEN_2_HYDROXYLASE_ACTIVITY** - likely <15 genes
- **GOMF_TESTOSTERONE_16_ALPHA_HYDROXYLASE_ACTIVITY** - likely <15 genes
- **GOMF_TESTOSTERONE_16_BETA_HYDROXYLASE_ACTIVITY** - likely <15 genes

These are **critical pathways for your study** (hormone metabolism), but were being excluded by `minsize = 15`.

### Why Different minsize for Pathways vs TFs?

- **Pathways** (minsize = 5):
  - GO Molecular Function terms can have very few genes
  - Hormone metabolism pathways are often small but specific
  - Want to capture specific enzymatic activities

- **TFs** (minsize = 20):
  - TF regulons are larger (multiple target genes)
  - Need sufficient targets to reliably estimate TF activity
  - Filters out poorly-characterized TFs

## Recommendation for Future

When setting `minsize` parameters:

1. **Check the original analysis** - Always verify what parameters were used in validated results

2. **Consider biology** - Think about typical gene set sizes for your domain:
   - GO Molecular Function: often small (5-50 genes)
   - GO Biological Process: medium (20-200 genes)
   - TF regulons: large (20-500 genes)

3. **Document choices** - Add comments explaining why a specific minsize was chosen

4. **Be conservative** - When in doubt, use smaller minsize to avoid missing important small gene sets. You can always filter results afterward by score threshold.
