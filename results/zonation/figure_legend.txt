Figure X, Panel B — Interaction between gonadal sex bias and hepatocyte zonation

B. Relationship between gonadal sex bias and spatial zonation bias in hepatocyte gene expression. Left: schematic illustrating the two axes of variation — gonadal sex bias (top; volcano plot of differential expression between testes- and ovaries-bearing mice) and hepatocyte zonation (bottom; periportal (PP) and pericentral (PC) compartments across ovaries (red) and testes (blue) conditions). Right: scatter plot of gonadal sex bias (y-axis; limma t-statistic, testes vs. ovaries) against zonation bias (x-axis; limma t-statistic, periportal vs. pericentral) for all hepatocyte-expressed genes. Positive y-values indicate ovary-biased expression; negative y-values indicate testes-biased expression. Positive x-values indicate periportal enrichment; negative x-values indicate pericentral enrichment. Dashed lines delineate significance thresholds. Genes in the corners of the plot exhibit both significant sex bias and zonation bias, revealing spatially restricted sex-dimorphic gene expression programmes. Selected genes with strong dual bias are labelled.
