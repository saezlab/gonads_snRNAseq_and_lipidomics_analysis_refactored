Figure X, Panels E,F — Cyp2d9 and Cyp2c38 knockdown validation

E,F. Validation of predicted gene–lipid associations by in vitro knockdown. Scatter plots show the relationship between in vivo gene–lipid correlation coefficients (x-axis; Pearson correlation between hepatocyte gene expression and lipid abundance across FCG mice, n = 43) and in vitro lipid perturbation effects (y-axis; limma t-statistic following siRNA-mediated knockdown in primary hepatocytes, n = 4 replicates) for Cyp2d9 (E) and Cyp2c38 (F). Each point represents one lipid species. Grey line and shaded area indicate linear regression fit with 95% confidence interval. Selected lipids with strong concordance between predicted and observed effects are labelled.
