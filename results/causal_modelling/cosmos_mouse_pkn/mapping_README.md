# Measured lipid crosswalk

Generated by `scripts/pkn/map_lipids.py` from the frozen September 8, 2026
SwissLipids download, the current 1,254-feature lipid DE result and the 64-column
Factor 1 correlation matrix. Original feature IDs remain unchanged.

The Python environment used was `/tmp/fcgl-pkn-goslin`; reproducible dependencies
are `pygoslin==2.2.5`, `scipy==1.13.1`, and `numpy==2.0.2`. Run
`python scripts/pkn/map_lipids.py` with those dependencies installed.
`--test-only` runs the nomenclature safeguards without the large source scan.
`--download` fetches SwissLipids if the frozen gzip is absent. The source is
Latin-1 encoded despite most identifiers and abbreviations being ASCII.

## Files

- `mapping_features.csv`: one row per original feature; naming level, parse
  problems, best mapping status, candidate counts, and ChEBI ID alternatives.
- `mapping_candidates.csv`: individual SwissLipids candidate entities, original
  and canonical names, evidence type, SwissLipids level, parents and class IDs.
- `mapping_swisslipids_catalog.csv`: compact source annotation for relevant
  matches and ancestors, plus all ChEBI-bearing SwissLipids records.
- `mapping_chebi_nodes.csv`: candidate ChEBI identity links, plus explicit Rhea
  pH 7.3 mappings where the identifier changes. The latter preserve whether the
  Rhea mapping was curated or computed. This file contains mappings, not edges.
- `mapping_metadata.json`: input hashes, source hash/release metadata, software
  versions, coverage summaries and limitations.

## Interpretation

`exact_reported_resolution` means that the canonical shorthand agrees at the
reported nomenclature resolution. It does **not** establish full molecular
identification, an unambiguous ChEBI record, a measured cellular compartment,
reaction specificity, or organism-specific occurrence. SwissLipids species
entries often describe chemically unresolved species and lack a ChEBI ID.

`compatible_lower_resolution` and `compatible_more_specific` retain the
direction of the resolution mismatch. `compatible_headgroup_isomer` is an
alternative sugar identity compatible with an unresolved HexCer or SHexCer
label. Glucose and galactose are not silently assigned.

`hierarchy_class` and `hierarchy_ancestor` are annotations. For candidate-derived
relations, `via_swisslipids_id` identifies one supporting candidate; ancestry
from a possible specific isomer does not establish that isomer as the measured
identity. These links must not be converted into signed causal edges. Candidate
lists are not exhaustive enumerations of every possible stereoisomer: detailed
isomers without ChEBI are omitted when they add resolution beyond a measurement.

Underscores are normalized by sorting unordered chains; slashes remain ordered.
Twenty-six two-moiety cardiolipin labels such as `CL(38:3_38:4)` are rejected by
the structural mapper because Goslin automatically reclassifies them as
dilysocardiolipin. They retain a broad cardiolipin annotation until their source
notation is verified. Unknown hydroxyl positions and oxygenation are not erased.

The final output has 877/1,254 features with exact reported-resolution SLM
matches, 163 with compatible matches only, 201 with class/ancestor annotations
only, and 13 unmapped. Only 128 features have any exact-resolution ChEBI match.
Of the 64 Factor 1 lipids, 41 have exact-resolution SLM matches, but only 3 have
exact-resolution ChEBI matches. Multiple resource records remain alternatives.
These are identifier-coverage counts, **not coverage by mechanistic reactions**.
